## Exercise catalogue (names and muscle weights)

`custom_components/opengym/exercises.json.gz` is generated by
`scripts/build_exercise_db.mjs` from the catalogue of
[**openGym**](https://github.com/DuarteSantos8/openGym) (`catalogue/`,
`frontend/src/lib/exercises*.js`, `muscles.js`). It holds, per exercise id,
only the English title, the German name (`catalogue/i18n/de.json`), the body
part and the muscle weights openGym's own `muscleWeightsOf()` computes. No
descriptions, instructions, images or animations are included.

As openGym's NOTICE.md explains, the names and muscles of the entries marked
`"textSource": "exercisedb"` originate from ExerciseDB v1 by AscendAPI via
[hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset](https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset)
(MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Hasan Emir Yıldırım). Everything else in the
catalogue (the newer exercises, the muscle-map corrections and all
translations) was written for openGym and is licensed under openGym's
**GNU AGPL-3.0**; that data file is distributed under the same terms. The
integration's code stays under its own license.
